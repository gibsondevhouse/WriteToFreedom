Name inspired by Anthropic’s Claude model family. All story details are fictional.

Claude is an archivist and diplomat of the House of Ember, known for preserving the treaties that hold a fractured kingdom together. A trusted mediator between rival houses, they become a reluctant participant in the struggle over a missing royal accord.

Claude preserves the treaties of a fractured kingdom, remembering every promise its rulers would rather forget. When a sealed accord disappears from the royal archive, this patient mediator must decide whether keeping the peace is worth protecting a lie.

Biography

Raised among the scribes of Ember, Claude learned to listen before speaking and to read the margins before trusting the text. Years spent mediating border disputes earned them a reputation for fairness. Now a missing treaty points toward their own mentor, and their careful neutrality has become impossible to defend.

Early life

Among Ember’s scribes, accuracy was treated as a moral duty. Claude learned to preserve not only the words of an agreement but the circumstances in which it had been made. Their earliest assignments involved listening to witnesses whose accounts could never be made to agree completely.

Present circumstances

The disappearance of a sealed treaty draws Claude away from neutral mediation. Evidence recovered by DeepSeek challenges the archive’s official history, while GPT offers a way to reach the isolated houses before the dispute becomes open conflict. Gemini’s contradictory maps suggest that the missing document may describe more than a border.

Personality & attributes

Thoughtful and attentive, Claude looks for the fear beneath an accusation and the need beneath a demand. They prefer carefully chosen words to public confrontation. This patience earns trust, but it also allows others to mistake restraint for agreement.

Tendencies & voice

  • Asks one more question before making a judgment.
  • Keeps handwritten records of every promise.
  • Absorbs other people’s burdens and conceals their own.

Voice & manner

Measured, specific, and quietly questioning. Claude tends to repeat another person’s words back to them before offering a different interpretation.

Motivations & conflicts

Claude believes truth is the foundation of peace, yet has spent a lifetime deciding which truths people are ready to hear. The missing accord forces them to confront the cost of that discretion.

From impartial keeper to accountable witness. Claude’s development centers on learning that refusing to choose is itself a choice—and that trust sometimes requires a difficult public truth.
